Pivoine is a whipped day cream made from fresh-pressed peony petals. All-day hydration, in one glass jar.

The flower

Picked at dawn,
when it's fullest.

We pick our peonies early in the morning, when the petals hold the most water, and press them the same day. The extract keeps everything the flower had.

  • Calmer toneRedness settles and the skin looks even by evening.

The cream is whipped light so it sits on top of the skin, then melts at body temperature and sinks in without leaving a film.
